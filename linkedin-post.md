## Posting tips for maximum reach

- **Timing:** Post Tuesday–Thursday, ~9–11 AM IST. Avoid weekends.
- **First 60 minutes matter most.** Reply to every comment quickly — early engagement
  tells the algorithm to show the post to more people.
- **Link placement:** Prefer Version 2 (link in first comment) for reach. If you use
  Version 1, it's simpler but may reach fewer people.
- **Turn on "Open to Work"** in your LinkedIn profile (recruiters-only or public banner).
- **Ask for reposts** — you already do in the post. A few reposts dramatically widen reach.
- **Tag thoughtfully:** mention 1–3 people or companies only if genuinely relevant.
  Over-tagging looks spammy and can hurt reach.
- **Add an image:** posts with a visual (e.g. a screenshot of your portfolio or the
  og-image card) get more engagement than text-only. Attach one before publishing.
- **Engage before & after:** comment on a few other posts ~30 min before and after
  publishing to warm up your reach.
